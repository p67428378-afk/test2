# Project Features

## SCRUM-99 - Warranty Tracker - Product Registration, Expiry Tracking & Service Claim History

### Feature Summary
A comprehensive Warranty Tracker allowing users to register products, store warranty terms, monitor expiry milestones, receive notification alerts, and log service and repair claim history.

### Key Features
- Product registration with purchase date, vendor, serial number, and receipt upload
- Automated warranty expiry calculation and status tracking (Active, Expiring Soon, Expired)
- Milestone notifications (30-day, 14-day, 1-day reminders)
- Service and warranty claim history logging with status and cost details
- Search, filtering, and detailed audit trail of all claim events
