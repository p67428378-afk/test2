# Project Features

## SCRUM-151 - Niche Job Board - Post Roles and Apply for Positions

### Feature Summary
A niche job board platform where employers can post open roles and job seekers can search, filter, and apply for positions by uploading resumes.

### Key Features
- Employer Role Posting: Employers can create, edit, and publish job postings with details like title, description, requirements, salary range, and location.
- Job Seeker Search and Filter: Job seekers can search for jobs by keywords, location, and filter by job type.
- Job Application Submission: Job seekers can apply for open roles by uploading a resume (PDF format, max 5MB) and providing a cover letter.
- Employer Application Management: Employers can view a list of applications for each of their job postings, download resumes, and update application status.
