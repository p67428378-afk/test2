# Project Features

## SCRUM-220 - Study Planner: Subject & Topic Management, Scheduling, Progress Tracking, and AI Recommendations

### Feature Summary
Allows students and self-learners to organize their study subjects and topics, schedule study blocks, track progress towards learning goals, and receive smart AI recommendations on what to study next.

### Key Features
- Subject & Topic Management (CRUD operations, duration estimation, difficulty levels)
- Customizable Study Schedules (date-based, recurring time blocks, daily goals)
- Progress Tracking & Analytics (completion status, session logging, subject progress bars)
- AI-Driven Topic Recommendations (smart prioritization based on completion history, difficulty, and upcoming deadlines)
