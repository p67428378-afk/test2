# Project Features

## SCRUM-200 - E-Commerce Exotic Chocolate Storefront & Order Management System

### Feature Summary
An e-commerce storefront allowing customers to explore exotic artisanal chocolates with rich origin/flavor details, manage a persistent shopping cart, and checkout securely with temperature-controlled delivery options.

### Key Features
- Interactive Product Catalog Browsing & Filtering by cocoa %, origin, flavor notes, dietary restrictions
- Detailed Product Pages with heat sensitivity notices & cold-pack recommendations
- Persistent Shopping Cart Management with real-time subtotal & shipping fee calculations
- Checkout & Temperature-Controlled Shipping (Standard Ground vs Express Thermal Shipping + Cold Pack)
- Order Confirmation & Tracking with UUID-based Order IDs
