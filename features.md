# Project Features

## SCRUM-123 - Hospital Management System - Core Patient, Appointment & Billing Management

### Feature Summary
A comprehensive web application that enables hospital administrators, medical staff, and patients to register and manage patient profiles, schedule doctor appointments, maintain medical records and prescriptions, and process billing invoices securely with role-based access control.

### Key Features
- Patient Registration & Records Management
- Doctor Scheduling & Appointment Booking
- Medical Records & Prescriptions Logging
- Billing & Invoicing with Payment Tracking
- User Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
