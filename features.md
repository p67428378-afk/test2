# Project Features

## SCRUM-206 - Hospital Management System

### Feature Summary
Provides hospital staff, doctors, administrators, and patients with a unified web portal to register patients, schedule consultations, manage clinical records, and process billing invoices efficiently.

### Key Features
- Patient Registration and Profile Management
- Doctor Scheduling and Slot Management
- Appointment Booking and Workflow Tracking
- Electronic Medical Records (EMR) & Clinical Notes
- Billing and Invoicing Management
